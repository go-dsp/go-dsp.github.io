Signal processing, in pure Go.
Designed like SciPy, checked against its answers.

go-dsp is the signal-processing layer of the Go scientific stack: filter design and filtering, spectral estimation and resampling, with the semantics of SciPy's scipy.signal. Its tests replay recorded SciPy answers, its documentation lists the error of every group of values in ULPs, and it says where it is slower. No cgo.

Why go-dsp

The scipy.signal calls, in Go.

Each function names the SciPy call it corresponds to and, where it differs, a note on MATLAB's. Option structs have useful zero values, and nothing panics on bad arguments: functions that can fail return an error.

〰️

IIR design

Butterworth, Chebyshev types 1 and 2, elliptic and Bessel filters, order selection, notch and peak filters, as zeros-poles-gain, transfer function or second-order sections.

🎚️

FIR design and windows

Window method, frequency-sampling, Parks–McClellan Remez, least squares, Kaiser order estimation, and more than twenty windows including Chebyshev and DPSS.

🔁

Filtering

LFilter, SOSFilt and their zero-phase forms FiltFilt and SOSFiltFilt, with initial conditions; convolution, correlation and overlap-add.

📊

Spectral estimation

Periodogram, Welch, cross-spectrum, coherence, spectrogram, STFT and its inverse, and Lomb–Scargle for unevenly sampled data.

⏩

Resampling

FFT and polyphase resampling, decimation and Upfirdn; the analytic signal with Hilbert; detrending.

⛰️

Peak finding

FindPeaks with height, threshold, distance, prominence, width and plateau conditions, and the prominence and width functions behind it.

📦

Pure Go, CGO=0

No cgo. The FIR kernels call the SIMD dot product of go-simd/floats; the transforms come from go-fft.

⚖️

BSD-3-Clause

Every repository of the organization. Ported code keeps its attribution in place.

Measured against SciPy, on one core.
